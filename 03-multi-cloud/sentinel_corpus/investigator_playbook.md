# Investigator Playbook for Fraud Investigation

## Overview

This playbook defines the standard operating procedure for SENTINEL investigators responding to flagged transactions. Every fraud alert generated by the SENTINEL risk engine must be triaged within four hours and investigated within 24 hours for amounts above ₹50,000. The investigation workflow follows a five-phase methodology: Triage, Evidence Collection, Pattern Analysis, Determination, and Remediation. Investigators must document every step in the SENTINEL Document DB to ensure a complete, auditable trail that satisfies RBI and NPCI regulatory requirements.

## Evidence Collection

Investigators must gather a comprehensive evidence package before proceeding to analysis. This includes: (1) the complete transaction history of the source account for the past 90 days, accessible via SENTINEL's vector search against historical embeddings; (2) device fingerprint data including IP address, device ID, operating system, and installed application list; (3) geolocation history from mobile carrier data or GPS logs associated with the account; (4) beneficiary account profiles, including the receiving bank, account age, and any prior fraud flags; and (5) communication records such as OTP logs, push notification receipts, and customer interaction transcripts from call centre recordings. All evidence must be collected using write-once-read-many (WORM) storage to prevent tampering, and cryptographic hashes must be recorded at the time of collection.

## Pattern Analysis and Determination

Using SENTINEL's ML-powered investigation assistant, investigators analyse the evidence for patterns consistent with known fraud typologies: account takeover (ATO), merchant fraud, UPI phishing, fake merchant onboarding, and money mule networks. The SENTINEL vector search engine identifies similar historical cases by embedding both the current evidence and the corpus of past investigations, returning the top 10 most similar cases with their determinations. Investigators then cross-reference NPCI rules and RBI guidelines from the SENTINEL corpus to verify regulatory compliance of any proposed action. Based on this analysis, the investigator assigns one of four determination codes: CONFIRMED_FRAUD, SUSPECTED_FRAUD, FALSE_POSITIVE, or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. Confirmed and suspected cases trigger automatic escalation to the senior fraud analyst.

## Remediation and Reporting

Upon determination, investigators execute the appropriate remediation: confirmed fraud cases require immediate account freeze, transaction reversal, and reporting to the Centralised Fraud Registry (CFR) within 24 hours. Suspected cases require enhanced monitoring on the account for 30 days and notification to the customer. Investigators must close the case in SENTINEL within 72 hours of determination, attaching all evidence, analysis notes, and the regulatory citations supporting the decision. Monthly aggregation of case outcomes feeds back into the SENTINEL model retraining pipeline, enabling continuous improvement of the risk engine. All investigation data remains in the system for a minimum of five years per RBI retention requirements.
